# Eagle Today100 — 2026-07-29

One-time evidence-first collection for Australian subclass 417 specified-work vacancies posted on 29 July 2026.

## Contract

- collect up to 100 individually identifiable vacancies from public SEEK pages
- retain the source job ID, individual URL, datePosted, exact location and description evidence
- use the official Home Affairs postcode tables for tourism/hospitality and regional specified industries
- reject closed listings, non-eligible metropolitan hospitality, required driver licence/own vehicle, unpaid work and explicit citizenship/PR restrictions
- hold unknown postcode, industry, accommodation, transport or required credentials
- never fabricate rows merely to reach 100
- no paid API or paid crawler
- collection produces artifacts only; Notion promotion occurs only after duplicate and schema review
- existing Final DB rows are never modified, archived or deleted

## CV efficiency

Vacancies are mapped to reusable evidence-based clusters rather than generating 100 cosmetically different resumes:

- Front Office / Reservations
- Operations / Site Administration
- Housekeeping / Accommodation Operations
- Hospitality All-Rounder / Food & Beverage
- Food Processing / Warehouse
- General Labour / Construction Support

Only APPLY NOW and high-priority VERIFY THEN APPLY rows receive CV mapping. No unverified licence, RSA, PMS, chef qualification or Australian experience is claimed.
