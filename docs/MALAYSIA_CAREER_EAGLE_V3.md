# Malaysia Career Eagle V3

## Operating decision

The former BPO-only track is retired as the primary Malaysia search model. The replacement searches across premium BPO, in-house travel/platform operations, fintech/payments, crypto/blockchain exchanges, risk/compliance operations, Trust & Safety, QA/SME/training, and partner/client operations.

The Australia Working Holiday Eagle V4 remains isolated and unchanged.

## Candidate advantage

The engine is optimized for a Korean-native candidate with 3+ years on the IGT Solutions / Agoda hotel and flight operation, including escalations, supplier negotiation, QA, SME/floor support, coaching, KPI/SLA work, high-volume cases and 24/7 shift reliability.

## Selection principle

A vacancy is not selected because it is merely Korean-speaking or BPO. It must show a realistic combination of:

1. Korean-language scarcity or Korean-market responsibility.
2. Direct transfer from Agoda customer/operations work.
3. Realistic seniority for a senior specialist/SME profile.
4. Malaysia Employment Pass or plausible authorization.
5. Salary at or above the project floor, unless salary is undisclosed and the in-house opportunity is strategically exceptional.
6. Career movement toward platform, fintech, crypto, risk, QA, training, partner operations or L1 operations analysis.
7. A live individual vacancy URL.

## Hard gates

- Closed, expired, duplicate or search/feed-only records.
- Malaysian/local-only work rights without sponsorship.
- Mandatory Mandarin/Chinese.
- Sales, commission, cold-calling, retail management, engineering or legal roles.
- Mandatory specialist credentials such as CAMS/ACAMS or a practicing legal certificate.
- Head/director/VP roles beyond verified management experience.
- Disclosed monthly salary below RM8,000.
- IGT Solutions by default. It can pass only when it is both a clear promotion and at least RM12,000 monthly.
- No verifiable Korean advantage.

## Final score

- Hiring reality: 30%
- Career transfer: 25%
- Salary: 20%
- Career upside: 15%
- Authorization and stability: 10%

Only A and B records enter the action shortlist. The shortlist is capped at 20 and the daily report surfaces the top 5. The engine does not publish invented employer hiring probabilities.
